PoE++ Type 4 / Class 8 delivers 90 W at the PSE and 71.3 W at the powered device, over a 41.1–57 V range. Set the device-side power as the hard limit your design may never exceed.

Power flows left→right. Tap any rail node (●) to probe its voltage and current on the scope. A converter with a constant-power loop is what keeps the source draw inside the PoE budget; a capacitor bank on its output rail supplies the crest-factor peaks.
